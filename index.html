Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

When there’s an opening, yes — but booking online or calling ahead guarantees your spot.

¿Hablan español?

¡Sí! Paty speaks English and Spanish. Switch this site to Spanish with the ES button at the top.

How long will my color appointment take?

The menu lists typical times. Big color changes can take longer — book a free consultation if you’re not sure.

Do you do quinceañera and bridal parties?

Yes. Book a trial and the day-of appointment, or call to plan hair for the whole party. Hair accessories are available at the salon.

What if I need to cancel?

Please call at least 24 hours ahead so another client can take the time.
