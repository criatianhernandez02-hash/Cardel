# Cardel Designs: brand kit

The full visual kit (logo options, how the brand looks on the website, Instagram and printed cards) is on the design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/9PWLLWit6QkncrZtBYLEL7

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Noir | `#121012` | Text, logo, dark sections |
| Cardel Red | `#B8102E` | Book buttons and accents only |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds |
| Garnet | `#8E0C24` | Hover / pressed red |
| Blush | `#F8ECEE` | Soft background panels |
| Smoke | `#5E5658` | Small secondary text |

Aim for roughly 60% white, 30% black and 10% red. Keep red for the things people should tap.

## Fonts (free, Google Fonts)

- **Playfair Display** is for headlines and the logo. Use it at weight 500, with *italic* for one or two accent words.
- **Jost** is for body text, buttons and labels (weights 400, 500 and 600). Small labels are set in capitals with wide letter spacing.

## Logo

- **Direction A**, "The signature", is recommended and used on the site: *Cardel* in italic Playfair Display over a red curve, with DESIGNS · HAIR SALON spaced out underneath.
- **The icon** is a red circle with a white italic "C". Use it for the profile picture, the browser tab icon, and anywhere the full logo would be smaller than 140 px wide.

## Voice

The voice is warm, personal and confident, written as Paty speaking. It's always bilingual: "Hair designed around you." / "Un look diseñado para ti."
